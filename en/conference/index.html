The conference

Digital childhood, in Oradea

„Impactul tehnologiei asupra dezvoltării emoționale, cognitive și a limbajului: soluții pentru părinți și cadre didactice” (“The impact of technology on emotional and cognitive development and on language: solutions for parents and teachers”). An afternoon for parents and teachers, at the University of Oradea, in 2026.

About the event

A call to awareness and to action

The conference was organised by prof. Ana-Felicia Codoban-Luca, a teacher at the Centrul Județean de Resurse și Asistență Educațională Bihor, the county centre for educational resources and assistance. The hall was full: several hundred parents and teachers.

„Această conferință s-ar putea să nu fie pe placul tuturor, deoarece în cadrul acesteia se vor trage câteva semnale de alarmă și vom aborda unele subiecte care vă pot scoate din zona de confort. Poate nu va fi un subiect confortabil, dar este unul necesar!”

“This conference may not be to everyone's liking, because in it a few alarms will be sounded and we shall take up some subjects that may bring you out of your comfort zone. It may not be a comfortable subject, but it is a necessary one!” Working translation from the Romanian.

From the opening presentation of the conference.

The aim, as the organiser put it: to inform, to raise awareness and to move people towards action.

The exact date of the conference is not recorded in the materials we have to hand; the year, 2026, appears on the slides of the event. We shall fill it in as soon as it is confirmed.

The closing quotation of the conference

„Copilul nu este un adult în miniatură, ci un adult în devenire, iar ceea ce va deveni depinde de ceea ce punem în el, de modelele care îl inspiră și mai ales de modul în care își petrece timpul.”

“A child is not a miniature adult but an adult in the making, and what he will become depends on what we put into him, on the models that inspire him and above all on the way he spends his time.” Working translation from the Romanian.

prof. Ana-Felicia Codoban-Luca, the closing slide of the conference.
