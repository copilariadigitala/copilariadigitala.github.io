One concrete step

The pledge of courageous parents

Read it, fill it in, tick what you are taking on, and print it. Everything you write here stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and we see none of it.

The original is in Romanian, titled Angajamentul părinților curajoși. What follows is a working translation, provided so that the pledge can be read and used in English; the Romanian original is here.

I take responsibility for guiding my child or children through a world in which screens, technology and information can shape children's hearts and minds. I choose to be their watchman, their guide and their protector.

Today! Now!

A few clarifications

The commitment until 16

The fourteenth point, the one about a smartphone and social networks until the age of 16, is a personal commitment, exactly as it appears in the original document. It is not the recommendation of a study and we do not present it as one. It is the line the author of the pledge drew for her own family, and offers to other parents.

What happens to what you fill in

Nothing leaves your browser. The text and the ticks are saved locally, in localStorage, so that you find them again if you close the page by accident. There is no server, no database, no form that gets submitted, no cookies and no analytics. The “Clear what I have filled in” button really does delete everything.

If JavaScript is disabled the page works just the same: the fields and the checkboxes are ordinary HTML, and printing is done from the browser's own menu (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P). The only thing missing is that nothing is remembered between visits.

The text

We have followed the document Angajamentul părinților curajoși as it was written. The corresponding slide in the conference presentation words it slightly differently; until the canonical version is confirmed, what stands here is the text of the document. This English version is a working translation of that Romanian text and has no separate standing.
