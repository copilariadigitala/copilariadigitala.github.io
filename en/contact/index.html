Invite us

To a school, a kindergarten, a parents' association

The conference was born for parents and teachers, and it can be given in other places too. If you would like it at your school, at your kindergarten or at a meeting of the parents' association, write to us, as soon as the address above exists.

Until then, the material is free to read on this site and free to print: the pledge can be copied and handed out at a parents' meeting, and the summary of the paper carries the full citations, so that you can check every statement for yourself.

Transparency

Who we are, for now

Copilăria Digitală is an awareness initiative started in Oradea by prof. Ana-Felicia Codoban-Luca, a teacher at the Centrul Județean de Resurse și Asistență Educațională Bihor, the county centre for educational resources and assistance, following the conference of the same name.

There is at this moment no registered association or foundation behind the initiative, and we do not claim that there is one. If that changes, it will be written here, with the name and the registration number.

What you will not find on this site

  • Cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts.
  • A form that sends your details somewhere. The pledge is filled in inside your own browser and stays there.
  • Figures without a source. Four of them are missing on purpose.

Have we got something wrong?

If you find a mistake, whether a wrong citation, a page number that does not match or a photograph you would rather we did not publish, tell us and we shall put it right. The site is public on GitHub as well, where every change stays visible in the history.
